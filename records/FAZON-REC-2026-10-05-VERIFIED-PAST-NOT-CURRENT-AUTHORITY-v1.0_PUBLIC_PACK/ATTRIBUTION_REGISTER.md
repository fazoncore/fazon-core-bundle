# Attribution register

This register records how third-party material is used in the frozen article and the limits of each attribution. It does not convert citation, discussion, or conceptual adjacency into endorsement, adoption, joint authorship, or technical equivalence.

## Ricky Jones
- Source: *Before the Story Decides*, 4 October 2026.
- Use in article: human-level framing about evidence, entitlement to act, incomplete information, and the point where a story begins to direct consequence.
- Boundary: the source is an essay about human judgment, not a technical control test and not an endorsement of FAZON.
- Source status: reviewed from owner-supplied screenshots; direct article permalink was not independently retrieved at freeze time.

## NIST / Rose, Borchert, Mitchell and Connelly
- Source: *Zero Trust Architecture*, NIST SP 800-207.
- Use in article: per-session access, dynamic policy, continual monitoring, and decision/enforcement separation.
- Boundary: does not establish FAZON novelty, product equivalence, or a mandatory live query for every action.

## IETF / Birkholz et al.
- Source: RFC 9334, *Remote ATtestation procedureS (RATS) Architecture*.
- Use in article: separation of Evidence, appraisal results, reliance, freshness, and bounded reuse.
- Boundary: does not make RATS attestation identical to FAZON post-action evidence or prove target consequence.
