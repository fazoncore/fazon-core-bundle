## Purpose

This public-safe record fixes the FAZON category position:

```text
Sector:
Enterprise AI Security and Governance

Category:
AI Execution Governance

Subcategory:
Pre-Execution Action Admissibility
```

The governed object is **the specific proposed action**.

The decision moment is **before an external or binding consequence**.

The public formula is:

> Permission before AI action becomes consequence.

The shorter operational formula is:

> No proof → no bind → no side effect.

## Scope

This record is a bounded category-position artefact. It distinguishes capability, identity and access, policy, evidence and assurance, monitoring, incident reporting, audit, and receipts from current action-specific permission.

→ specific proposed action
