# Public Definitions

## Enterprise AI Security and Governance

The broad sector containing security, identity, policy, risk, evidence, assurance, monitoring, audit, transparency, incident reporting, and related enterprise controls for AI systems.

## AI Execution Governance

The FAZON category position concerned with whether consequential AI-driven actions may proceed under current governance conditions.

## Pre-Execution Action Admissibility

The bounded subcategory in which a specific proposed action is evaluated immediately before an external or binding consequence may occur.

## Specific Proposed Action

The exact action under consideration, including its relevant actor or agent, purpose, scope, target, context, evidence basis, and specified consequence.

## Current Authority

The attributable authority applicable to the exact proposed action under present conditions. Historical approval or stored access does not automatically establish current authority.

## Evidence-Grade Receipt

A public category-level term for evidence preserving the relationship between a governance decision and the observed consequence—or, following a non-permit outcome, evidence supporting confirmed non-occurrence of the governed side effect.

## Execution Boundary

A conceptual decision boundary before an external or binding consequence. This public record does not claim a particular deployed technical implementation.
