## Purpose

This public-safe record fixes a bounded governance distinction:

> A verified past is not authority to act now.

Past evaluation, approval, provenance, reputation, or other historical evidence may remain relevant evidence. They do not, by themselves, establish that a specific consequential action is authorized under the authority, scope, conditions, and material parameters that apply now.

The article also uses the human-level distinction that social trust may inform judgment without silently propagating authority.
